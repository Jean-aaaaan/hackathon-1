What Vantage Is

Vantage is a per-account AI agent platform. One agent runs continuously against every HubSpot deal. It monitors signals 24/7, builds a living intelligence file on each account, generates drafted emails, and plans the next sales actions — without the rep having to ask.

The goal is to replace the mental overhead of pipeline management. Rather than a rep manually reviewing 376 deals to decide what to do next, Vantage surfaces a ranked, context-aware action queue every morning with everything pre-prepared.

The Agent Pipeline

Every night (2am), a pipeline of 7 stages runs sequentially for every deal in the workspace. The pipeline runs in parallel across 5 deals at a time. Each stage receives the previous stage's output, so intelligence compounds through the chain. After the pipeline, a separate rules-based Plays Engine fires additional drafts based on detected plays (champion dark, close date at risk, etc.).

Data Sources

The pipeline consumes four live data streams per deal:

  • HubSpot — deal fields, contact activity, email log, stage history, engagement metrics
  • Fireflies.ai — call transcripts matched to deals by email domain + calendar time
  • Perplexity (sonar-pro) — real-time web intelligence: funding, leadership changes, news, incidents
  • Outlook Calendar — upcoming meetings matched to deals for pre-meeting prep actions

MEDDPICC Framework

MEDDPICC is the primary qualification framework. Every deal is scored across eight components on every pipeline run. The scores drive urgency, risk detection, action creation, and POV generation.

⚠

Most common misclassification: Economic Buyer vs Champion. The EB controls budget and can approve alone. The champion believes in you and sells internally. Confusing them is the leading cause of late-stage deal loss — the EB surfaces at the last moment and says no. The agent flags this explicitly when a person is misclassified.

MEDDPICC Gap to Action Mapping

When any component score falls below 0.35, the Gap Fill engine creates a targeted action. The three most commonly triggered:

👤

EB Gap

Creates a stakeholder_intro action — find and engage the budget owner before the deal advances further

📋

DC Gap

Creates an email action — send a decision criteria alignment brief showing how Invigilo maps to their evaluation framework

💰

IP Gap

Creates a call_prep action — book an ROI conversation to quantify pain. Intent without quantified pain stalls at proposal stage

3 Whys Framework

Layered on top of MEDDPICC, the 3 Whys framework tests whether the deal has a compelling reason to exist. The Risk Scanner evaluates each "Why" against the deal's evidence and flags gaps in the War Room POV panel.

🔄

Why Change

What forces them off the status quo. Safety incident, regulatory audit, board mandate, cost of manual processes. Without this, the deal stalls — they have no reason to move.

⏱

Why Now

What creates urgency in this quarter specifically. Budget cycle closing, new regulation deadline, recent incident, new HSQE hire. Without this, deals slip quarter after quarter.

🏆

Why Us

Why Invigilo over Voxel, Intenseye, Protex AI. SafeKey's GA Grounding Agent, ISO 27001, no camera upgrade needed, on-premise AI for MENA data residency. Without this, the deal goes to a cheaper competitor.

All three must be evidenced for a deal to be considered fully qualified. Missing any one of the three is flagged as a risk in the POV and surfaces a targeted action.

Scoring Algorithms

Urgency Score (0–1)

Computed by the Prioritiser agent (Sonnet 4.6). The primary sort key for the inbox. Drives which accounts get the intraday refresh sweep and populate the "30 critical" counter in the status bar.

Composite of: MEDDPICC gap severity, risk vector levels, days since last meaningful activity, close date proximity, signal count and recency. Sonnet synthesises these into a calibrated 0–1 score — not a rigid formula, but a trained judgment call.

Health Score (0–1)

Separate from urgency. Measures deal momentum rather than immediate risk. Inputs: engagement trend (improving / flat / declining), days in current stage versus pipeline average, response rate, multi-threading depth. A deal can be both high urgency (close soon, action needed) and high health (engagement is strong). Or low urgency and low health — a stalled deal with no near-term close date.

ICP Fit Score (0–1)

The ResearcherAgent scores each deal against Invigilo's Ideal Customer Profile on every run.

TierScore rangeCharacteristics
Strong0.75 – 1.0Industrial operations, existing CCTV, HSE Director engaged, MENA/APAC, compliance pressure
Moderate0.50 – 0.74Right industry but missing 1–2 ICP factors (e.g. no existing cameras, or smaller site count)
Weakbelow 0.50Office/retail without industrial operations, no CCTV, consumer-facing
Unknown—Insufficient data to score. Agent flags what evidence is missing.

Win Probability

Derived from MEDDPICC component scores and risk vectors. Higher qualification coverage × lower risk = higher probability. Used in the Forecast page to categorise deals into four buckets:

  • Commit — high confidence, all MEDDPICC covered, low risk
  • Best Case — likely but has ≥1 risk vector at high/critical
  • Pipeline — early stage or significant gaps
  • Omit — stalled, dark, or strong competitor in evaluation

Focus Score (Action Queue)

Used to select the top 7 actions for "Today's Focus" from all pending queue items. Calculated as:

focus_score = urgency × 0.5 + amount_normalised × 0.2 + overdue_bonus + priority × 0.3

Where amount_normalised caps at $200K (prevents mega-deals dominating) and overdue_bonus = +0.30 for any action past its due date.

Self-Driving Action Engine (Gap Fill)

After the five AI agents run, the Gap Fill engine runs as a pure rules engine — no LLM call, no cost. It inspects the deal's current timeline and inserts the minimum set of actions needed to keep the deal moving. Maximum 3 upcoming actions per deal at any time.

Each action type has a cooldown — the same action type cannot be re-created within its cooldown window, preventing alert fatigue.

Rule 1 — Close Push

Close date within 30 days

Creates a close_push action due tomorrow. Confirm commit or agree on extension. Cooldown: 7 days.

Rule 2 — Champion Dark

No outbound contact in 14+ days

Creates a champion_checkin action due today. Re-engage before the deal goes cold. Cooldown: 5 days.

Rule 3 — Touchpoint Gap

No planned action for 7+ days

Creates a champion_checkin action due in 3 days. Regular contact prevents deals from drifting. Cooldown: 5 days.

Rule 4 — MEDDPICC Gap

EB / DC / IP score below 0.35

Creates a targeted action per gap component. Action type and wording are gap-specific. Cooldown: 14 days.

Rule 5 — Critical Signal

Any signal with urgency ≥ 0.85

Creates a signal_trigger action due today (status: overdue). Two guards prevent false creation. Cooldown: 2 days.

Auto-Resolve

ResearcherAgent marks signal resolved

Open actions for resolved signal types are auto-completed. No rep action needed. The agent reads the full email thread and call context to determine this.

Rule 5 Guards (avoiding stale actions)

Rule 5 has two layered checks before creating a signal_trigger action:

  1. Resolved signal check — if the ResearcherAgent's resolved_signals list includes this signal type (populated from the full email thread and call transcript context), skip entirely.
  2. Outbound interaction check — if there is any outbound email, call, or meeting recorded in the interactions table after the signal was created, skip — the rep already acted on it.
✓

Example: Sembcorp Solar's "legal_review_started" signal fired when their DPA was requested. Vishnu signed and emailed "I have signed the document." The ResearcherAgent reads this, populates resolved_signals: ["legal_review_started"], and Gap Fill auto-completes the existing action rather than re-surfacing it.

Action Sources

Timeline actions are created from four sources, tracked via the source field:

  • agent_gap_fill — nightly pipeline rules engine (Rules 1–4)
  • signal_trigger — high-urgency signal (Rule 5)
  • fireflies_action_item — extracted from call transcript action items (webhook)
  • calendar_match — upcoming Outlook meeting → pre-meeting prep action
  • rep_created — manually added by the rep in War Room

Grounding — Gold Data Layer

The Grounding Agent is Vantage's key differentiator versus competitors like Actively AI (whose grounding is in beta behind a feature flag). Every fact surfaced by the ResearcherAgent is verified before it reaches the rep:

  • Each fact is tagged with its source (HubSpot field, email, Fireflies transcript, Perplexity article) and a confidence score
  • Conflicting facts across sources (e.g. close date in HubSpot field ≠ close date mentioned in email) are flagged explicitly
  • The Grounding Agent resolves conflicts by applying source hierarchy: real-time activity > CRM notes > inferred from pattern
  • The Audit Panel in the War Room makes every fact and its source visible to the rep — nothing is opaque

Transcript and Calendar Matching

Linking a Fireflies call recording to the right HubSpot deal is a non-trivial matching problem — the same sales rep is on many calls, and deal names in HubSpot rarely match transcript titles. Vantage uses a four-tier scoring system with a minimum threshold of 2 to match.

ScoreSignal typeExampleNotes
+4Calendar time matchOutlook event at 14:00 SGT, Fireflies transcript at 14:02 SGTDefinitive — one meeting can only happen once. Eliminates same-contact-multiple-deals ambiguity.
+2Email domain match@westgold.com.au participant → "Westgold" in deal nameNon-seller domains only. Seller domains (invigilo.sg, gmail.com) are excluded.
+2Title word match"Westgold ~ Invigilo AI" in transcript titleOnly specific words count. Generic words (India, Singapore, Demo, Group, Limited) are excluded from matching.
+1Date proximityTranscript date within deal's active windowTiebreaker only — never sufficient alone. Requires base score ≥ 1.
⚠

Why generic words are excluded: "India Glycols" and "Coca Cola India" both contain the word "india." Any transcript discussing the Indian market would match both. The matcher blocks geographic and descriptive words from being the sole match criterion — only company-specific proper nouns count.

Calendar as Primary Anchor

Calendar time matching (±15 minutes) is the only signal strong enough to definitively resolve same-contact-multiple-deals ambiguity. When a rep is on 10 active deals and talks to Vishnu in every one, email domain alone cannot differentiate. A specific timestamp can. This path activates automatically once Outlook is connected — no configuration required.

The Learning Loop

Vantage is designed to get more accurate over time through a structured feedback loop. Two feedback channels feed back into the agent pipeline:

Draft Acceptance Rate (DAR)

Every drafted email the rep reviews has one of four outcomes: approved, approved with edits, declined with reason, or pushed to HubSpot. The DAR is the ratio of approvals to total drafts reviewed, tracked as a 30-day rolling metric in Analytics.

Decline Reason Codes

When a draft is declined, the rep selects a reason:

  • Wrong tone — voice or formality doesn't match the relationship
  • Wrong timing — right message, wrong moment
  • Already sent — the rep already handled it
  • Wrong content — factually incorrect or misses the point
  • Not relevant — the signal that triggered this draft is stale

These reason codes become Interaction records with is_training_signal = true. The ResearcherAgent reads recent training signals when building context for future runs on that deal, making the next draft progressively more calibrated.

Voice Profile

The DrafterAgent is calibrated to the rep's writing style by analysing outbound emails from the Outlook sent folder. Extracted dimensions: sentence length, formality register, opening and closing patterns, use of bullet points versus prose. Drafts are written in the rep's voice — not generic AI tone. The profile can be re-analysed from Settings → Voice Profile at any time.

Episodic Memory

Each deal accumulates a running interaction history: HubSpot emails, Fireflies call transcripts, calendar meetings, rep notes, declined draft reasons, completed actions. The ResearcherAgent reads the most recent interactions on every run. The agent never starts from zero — it knows what was said last week, what was promised on the last call, and what emails bounced.

💡

The compounding effect: A deal that's been in the pipeline for 3 months has 3 months of interaction history, transcript context, and decline feedback. The agent's output on that deal is significantly more accurate than on a deal created yesterday. DAR improves as the episodic memory accumulates — the Week 8 target reflects this ramp-up curve.
