The Problem

389 deals. 3–18 month cycles. One team.

You cannot give every deal proper attention manually. Champions go dark. Close dates slip. Proposals land in inboxes during leave windows. The ones that matter get lost in the noise.

🔇

Champions go silent

In long enterprise cycles, the person driving your deal internally goes quiet for weeks. By the time you notice, a competitor has moved in.

📅

Close dates drift unnoticed

A $1M deal can sit at Proposal stage for 59 days past its close date while the CRM still shows it as "on track." No one flags it.

⏰

Follow-up timing matters

The right email after a site visit, sent the day a champion returns from leave, is 10x more effective than the same email sent a week late.

How It Works

7 stages, every night, every deal.

At 2am, Vantage runs a 7-stage AI pipeline across every active deal in HubSpot. Each stage has one job. The chain runs in sequence — researcher feeds grounding, grounding feeds prioritiser, prioritiser feeds drafter. After the pipeline, a separate Plays Engine fires additional drafts for named situations (champion dark, close date at risk). Every output is code-verified before it reaches you.

Quality Control

Safety gates. Code-enforced, not prompt-enforced.

The model cannot override these. They run in Python after every pipeline, before any draft reaches your inbox.

🔢

Hard cap at 2 drafts

No matter what the model returns, only the top 2 drafts per account per night are shown. Quality over volume.

📡

Zero signals = no outbound drafts

If Grounding Agent found nothing verified, outbound emails are suppressed entirely. No hallucination risk.

🤝

No phantom champion re-engagement

Champion MEDDPICC must be above 0.2 before a champion re-engagement draft is generated. You can't re-engage someone you've never engaged.

💬

No ROI email before first reply

If the buyer has never replied, ROI business cases and executive alignment emails are blocked. Discovery comes first.

📊

Confidence capped by health

A 0.06-health deal cannot produce a 0.90-confidence draft. Confidence ceiling = health score + 0.25.

🚫

Sender stripped from stakeholders

Before drafting, the pipeline removes your own name from the stakeholder list. No self-addressed emails.
